# Formula Bar in TypeScript Spreadsheet

The formula bar is used to enter or edit cell data more easily. By default, the formula bar is enabled in the spreadsheet. Use the [`showFormulaBar`](https://ej2.syncfusion.com/documentation/api/spreadsheet#showformulabar) property to enable or disable the formula bar.
